## Discovery architecture
Every platform should use the same recognizable name, profile image, visual language, handle where available, and official website URL. Cross-link Instagram, YouTube and website. Keep the exact spelling HOWLREX prominent while naturally including “Howl Rex” in descriptions where useful.

## YouTube operating system
Use three formats:
1. Shorts: discovery and repeatable hooks.
2. Cinematic films: brand depth and storytelling.
3. Behind-the-scenes / journey: relationship and returning viewers.

Packaging:
- Put the most important searchable words near the beginning of titles.
- Use custom thumbnails with one dominant subject and minimal readable text.
- Make the first seconds deliver the promise of the title/thumbnail.
- Build recurring series so viewers recognize the format.
- Use Analytics to compare impressions, CTR, retention, traffic sources and returning viewers.
- Link Shorts to relevant long-form videos when appropriate.

Suggested recurring series:
- HOWLREX // ARRIVAL
- HOWLREX // AFTER DARK
- HOWLREX // DRIVE
- HOWLREX // THE FRAME
- HOWLREX // JOURNAL
- HOWLREX // BEHIND THE SCENE

## Instagram operating system
Content pillars:
- Cinematic model portraits
- Automotive/luxury sequences
- Short cinematic reels
- Behind-the-scenes
- Personal journey / discipline
- Cross-platform teasers

Reel structure:
0–2 sec: strongest visual/hook
2–7 sec: movement/story
7–12 sec: escalation/payoff
final beat: recognizable HOWLREX identity + simple CTA

Use original footage whenever possible. Keep cover images visually consistent with YouTube thumbnails and the website.

## Website conversion system

Instagram → Website
YouTube → Website
Website → Instagram + YouTube

Keep official social links visible and make the website title/description consistently identify HOWLREX.

## 30-day publishing cadence
YouTube:
- 3 Shorts/week
- 1 cinematic long-form/video every 1–2 weeks
- 1 community post/week when available

Instagram:
- 4–5 Reels/week
- 2–3 photo/carousel posts/week
- Stories on active shoot/publish days

Repurpose one shoot into:
1 hero image
1 Reel
2–4 Shorts
1 BTS clip
1 YouTube cinematic cut
1 website journal entry

## Measurement
Do not optimize for views alone. Track:
- Reach/impressions
- Click-through rate where available
- Average view duration
- Audience retention
- Shares/saves
- Profile visits
- Followers/subscribers gained per content piece
- Website clicks

Then produce more of the formats/topics that demonstrate strong audience response.

## Important
No platform can guarantee virality or a particular ranking. The system is designed to maximize discoverability, consistency, viewer satisfaction and cross-platform conversion without artificial engagement or spam.
